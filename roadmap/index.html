VERSION 4.0 · NEXT MILESTONE NEXT UP AFTER VERSION 3.2

True Offline-First PWA & Multi-Device /install/ Hub

Following the completion of Version 3.2, our next major release transforms Plantipedia into an installable, resilient Offline-First Progressive Web App built for forests, classrooms, smart boards, and low-connectivity botanical fieldwork.

  • 📴 True Offline-First Service Worker (sw.js): Full pre-caching of all 50 curated 15-chapter monographs, stylesheets, taxonomic directories, and bilingual search indexes for 100% internet-free botanical fieldwork.
  • 💻 Multi-Device /install/ Hub: One-Click Windows Official App, portable `.exe` / `.apk` packages, guided iOS Safari 2-step home-screen flow, and distraction-free Smart Board Kiosk mode.
  • 🔄 Background Auto-Update Toast: Automatic cache invalidation with a sleek one-click Update Now notification banner whenever new botanical releases ship.
VERSION 4.1 FIELD HARDWARE STUDIO

Garden QR Tag Studio & Waterproof Botanical Nameplates

  • Print-Ready SVG & PDF Placards: Generates crisp garden plaques combining Latin binomials, Bengali script, family clade, and scannable monograph QR links.
  • Customizable Campus & Arboretum Layouts: Supports weatherproof aluminum-etch ratios, stake margins, and batch export for botanical gardens and school herbariums.
  • Direct Live Server Tagging: Encode QR links pointing either to local 15-chapter monographs or live Global Botanical Server species dossiers.
VERSION 4.2 IDENTIFICATION ENGINE

Botanical Akinator — Interactive Dichotomous Key

  • 20-Question Morphological Diagnostic Tree: Identifies unknown wild or cultivated specimens through simple phyllotaxy, leaf margin, petal count, latex, and habitat prompts.
  • Confidence Scoring & Ranked Candidates: Narrow down candidate species dynamically with transparent trait-match percentages and instant comparison links.
  • Bilingual Novice & Scholarly Prompts: Clear visual trait hints alongside classical botanical terminology linked to Plantipedia's interactive glossary.
VERSION 4.3 EDGE-CASE HARDENING & TELEMETRY

Edge-Case Hardening, Phenological Telemetry & Herbarium Exports

  • Edge-Case Hardening & Resilience Audits: Exhaustive validation of rare homonyms, missing taxonomic ranks, cross-subpath link integrity, and WCAG AAA keyboard navigation benchmarks.
  • Cross-Biome Telemetry Visualizations: Interactive soil pH, USDA/Bengal thermal envelope, and seasonal bloom-overlap heatmaps across saved My Herbarium collections.
  • Portable Herbarium Bundles: Export curated study sets with APA/MLA/BibTeX citations and offline-ready A4 field sheets in a single archive package.
VERSION 3.2 · SHIPPED & CURRENT ACTIVE BUILD COMPLETED · CHRONOLOGICAL ARCHIVE (v1.0 → v3.2)

6-Source Federated Engine, Global Fuzzy Search & 🎲 Surprise Specimen

Version 3.2 marks the completion of Plantipedia's multi-source parallel botanical architecture—synthesizing live records across GBIF Backbone, Global Flora Index, Wikidata, iNaturalist, Encyclopedia of Life, and Open Tree of Life while delivering zero-key EN / বাংলা translation, typo-tolerant worldwide fuzzy search, emerald match highlighting, `/` and `Esc` keyboard shortcuts, and the 3-second `🎲 Surprise Specimen` botanical discovery modal.

  • Multi-Source Parallel Resolution (1,600,000+ Taxa): Queries resolve concurrently across 6 open botanical registries with multi-angle verified specimen galleries and biome-calibrated telemetry.
  • Federated Global Fuzzy Search & Instant Emerald Highlighting: Client-side Levenshtein matching paired with parallel Plantae-scoped fuzzy APIs recovers typos across worldwide species while highlighting matches cleanly.
  • 3-Tier Zero-Key Translation, `/` Shortcuts & 🎲 Surprise Specimen: Seamless EN / বাংলা dossier toggling, instant `/` search focus & `Esc` overlay dismissal, and a 3-second specimen tumbler discovery sequence.
