🏷️ Garden QR Tag Studio — Arboretum & Botanical Nameplate Generator

Scheduled for Plantipedia Version 4.1, the Garden QR Tag Studio is currently undergoing precision print-layout development. Schools, campus gardens, home growers, and botanical arboretums will be able to compose bilingual EN / বাংলা botanical nameplates paired with high-contrast QR codes pointing directly to Plantipedia's 15-chapter monographs or live Global Botanical Server pages.

STUDIO MODULE 01

Botanical Nameplate Composer

Formats English name, Bengali script, italicized Linnaean binomial, plant family, and native origin into clean typographic plaque layouts with zero overlap.

STUDIO MODULE 02

Instant QR Placard Preview

Generates error-corrected vector QR matrices bound to canonical https://plantipedia.rayaantasnim.workers.dev/ monograph URLs for fast smartphone scanning outdoors.

STUDIO MODULE 03

Waterproof Label Export Specs

Supports A4 multi-tag print sheets, 100×65mm engraved plaque SVG ratios, and UV-resistant laminated stake templates with high-contrast monochrome ink modes.
